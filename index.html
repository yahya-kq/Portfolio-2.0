Financial Analysis Report

Khyber Tobacco Company Limited

Sector: Tobacco Manufacturing Coverage: FY2020 – FY2025 FY25 Revenue: Rs. 9,891M (+217.7%) Z-Score / Solvency: 1.26 (Distress Zone)
Download PDF Excel Model

Executive Summary

Khyber Tobacco Company Limited (KTC), a Mardan-based cigarette and tobacco manufacturer listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange under ticker KHTC, experienced substantial volatility across FY2020–FY2025. Net sales expanded from Rs. 1,829 million in FY2020 to Rs. 7,435 million in FY2023, contracted 58.1 percent to Rs. 3,114 million in FY2024, and rebounded 217.7 percent to a record Rs. 9,891 million in FY2025. FY2021 and FY2024 recorded operating and net losses, while the FY2025 recovery closed with a net profit margin of 2.8 percent following an extensive balance sheet expansion funded primarily through trade credit.

Company Overview

Incorporated in 1954 and headquartered in Mardan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, KTC manufactures cigarettes, filter rods, and re-dried tobacco products. The company distributes domestically under the Gold Street Classic and Gold Street International brands and exports to markets across the Middle East, Eastern Europe, South and West Africa, and Central and South Asia. Total assets expanded over sixfold from Rs. 2,602 million in FY2020 to Rs. 16,723 million in FY2025, driven by significant inventory build-up.

Financial Performance & Margins

Revenue fluctuations generated wide swings in gross profit margins: 17.4 percent in FY2020, rising to a peak of 37.3 percent in FY2023, compressing to 5.0 percent in FY2024, and recovering to 17.9 percent in FY2025. Operating performance reflects notable operating leverage; administrative and distribution costs represented 27.7 percent of sales during the FY2024 contraction compared to 7.0 percent in FY2025. Net profit after tax mirrored this pattern, shifting from a loss of Rs. 1,022 million in FY2024 to a profit of Rs. 275 million in FY2025.

Net Sales, Rs. Million (FY2020–FY2025)

Liquidity & Leverage Structure

Working capital metrics reflect significant operational and balance sheet pressure. The current ratio closed FY2025 at 1.04x, while the quick ratio deteriorated to 0.26x as stock in trade expanded to Rs. 10,340 million, constituting 61.8 percent of total assets. Days inventory outstanding reached 465 days in FY2025 compared to 161 days in FY2020. Asset growth was financed overwhelmingly through trade payables (Rs. 13,395 million in current liabilities), lifting the debt-to-equity ratio to 4.70x and reducing equity to 17.6 percent of total assets.

Key Findings & Strengths

  • Export-driven revenue capability with net sales expanding by 217.7 percent in FY2025 to a record Rs. 9,891 million.
  • Improved trade receivables turnover reaching 10.33x (35 days sales outstanding) in FY2025 from 2.31x in FY2024.
  • Manufacturing capital investments with property, plant, and equipment expanding from Rs. 819 million to Rs. 2,737 million.
  • Absence of substantial long-term funded debt, keeping non-current liabilities below 5 percent of total assets.

Key Vulnerabilities & Risks

  • Liquidity compression with quick ratio at 0.26x, leaving debt service dependent on inventory conversion.
  • Leverage concentration in short-term trade payables resulting in a 4.70x debt-to-equity ratio.
  • Inventory holding cycles averaging 465 days, tying up working capital and elevating carrying risk.
  • Altman Z-Score residing in the Distress Zone (1.26 in FY2025; distress in four of six years).

Conclusion & Assessment

KTC has demonstrated commercial scale in international export markets, yet revenue expansion has coincided with rising balance sheet vulnerability. Sustaining operational health requires accelerating inventory turnover, stabilizing export demand cycles, and gradually recapitalizing the equity base to reduce supplier credit dependency.

Web Application · Personal Finance

WealthNest

Platform: Browser-Based (No Install) Stack: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js Database & Auth: Supabase (PostgreSQL + RLS) Hosting: Vercel
🚀 Live App Get Code

Core Purpose

WealthNest is a browser-based personal finance tracker designed to help individuals monitor income, manage expenses, set category budgets, and track savings goals in one central place. The application requires no bank account connection and no software installation, offering a clean and private environment to manage personal finances.

Key Features

  • Dashboard: Real-time monthly summary displaying total income, expenses, net savings, and current budget utilization at a glance.
  • Transaction Logging: Detailed tracking of income, expenses, and savings contributions with customizable categories and subcategories.
  • Monthly Budgets: Spending limits defined per category with automated progress tracking and visual indicators.
  • Savings Goals: Milestone tracking with target monetary amounts, deadlines, and remaining balance calculations.
  • Visual Reports: Interactive category breakdowns and month-over-month comparisons powered by Chart.js.

Data Storage and Privacy

The application offers two flexible modes of operation. In local demo mode, all financial data is stored directly in the user's browser, enabling full functionality offline without creating an account. For multi-device access, users can sign in using email or Google authentication to sync records to a secure Supabase PostgreSQL database. Each user's records are protected by Row Level Security policies, ensuring data can only be accessed by the account owner. Users can also permanently delete their account and all associated data directly within settings.

Monte Carlo Risk Simulator

Overview & Purpose

Monte Carlo simulation is a mathematical technique used in quantitative finance to model the probability of different outcomes in the presence of random variables. The platform generates thousands of potential future price paths for multi-asset equity portfolios using historical return statistics and empirical covariance structures.

This workstation operates strictly as a downside risk modeling platform. It measures capital exposure, tail loss boundaries, and drawdown probabilities under extreme market scenarios. The simulation does not predict future stock prices or forecast investment returns; its mandate is to provide portfolio managers and risk officers with verifiable metrics of potential monetary loss at defined confidence thresholds.

Control Panel & Parameter Configuration

The parameter sidebar provides complete institutional control over asset selection and simulation engine settings:

  • Market Universes: Dynamic toggle between United States Equities (denominated in USD, 252-day annual trading calendar) and Pakistan Stock Exchange (denominated in PKR, 250-day annual trading calendar). Switching markets updates the ticker registry and covariance matrix in real time.
  • Asset Universe: Ten liquid blue-chip equities per market. Users can select any combination of assets across banking, technology, energy, and industrial sectors.
  • Capital & Weight Allocation: Custom portfolio weights with automated proportional normalization guaranteeing an exact 100% allocation across selected assets. Flexible starting capital inputs (e.g., $100,000 USD or Rs. 10,000,000 PKR).
  • Simulation Scope: Path counts configurable from 1,000 up to 50,000 iterations for high-precision tail estimation. Forecast horizons from 1 trading day up to 252 trading days (1 full trading year).
  • Confidence Intervals & Auditability: Risk evaluation selectable at 90%, 95%, and 99% confidence thresholds. Deterministic random seed control enables exact reproducibility for backtesting and compliance verification.

Analytical Workstation Modules

The analytical interface evaluates risk profiles across four specialized modules:

  • Loss Distribution & Tail Risk: Empirical return histogram fitted against parametric normal distributions. Color coded risk zones isolate Value at Risk thresholds and the Conditional Value at Risk average loss zone. Includes cumulative distribution functions (CDF) and empirical higher moments (skewness, excess kurtosis, Jarque-Bera normality tests).
  • Asset Trajectories & Spatial Manifold: Multi-line fan charts tracking wealth trajectories across 5th, 25th, median (50th), 75th, and 95th percentile confidence bands. Hardware-accelerated Three.js surface canvas renders simulated trajectories in an interactive 3D spatial manifold alongside pairwise Pearson correlation heatmaps.
  • Multi-Portfolio Scenario Comparison: Side-by-side benchmarking of active allocations against Aggressive Growth, Balanced Core, and Capital Preservation institutional profiles, supported by comparative risk scorecards and risk-versus-return frontier plots.
  • Model Lineage & Mathematical Specification: Complete transparency into mathematical differential equations, data feed origin timestamps, and underlying assumptions regarding covariance stationarity.

Mathematical Framework & Simulation Mechanics

The simulation engine models asset dynamics using multi-asset Geometric Brownian Motion coupled through Cholesky factorization:

  • Log Returns & Covariance Structure: Computes daily continuous log returns ri,t = ln(Pi,t / Pi,t-1) across historical price series to construct the asset mean vector (μ) and sample covariance matrix (Σ).
  • Cholesky Factorization: Factors the positive semi-definite covariance matrix into Σ = L · LT, where L is a lower triangular matrix. Correlated shocks are generated via ε = L · Z, where Z represents independent standard normal random variates, preserving the exact empirical correlation matrix.
  • Discrete GBM Formulation: Trajectories are projected daily using Ito's lemma formulation: Si(t + Δt) = Si(t) · exp((μi - 0.5σi2)Δt + σi√Δt · εi), where the -0.5σi2 term prevents compounding upward drift bias.
  • Coherent Tail Risk Measurement: Evaluates Value at Risk (VaRα) at confidence level α alongside Conditional Value at Risk (CVaRα = E[Loss | Loss ≥ VaRα]). CVaR measures expected losses beyond the VaR threshold, penalizing tail risk and asymmetric downside distributions.

Data Pipeline & Architectural Engineering

Live adjusted closing prices and trading volume for US equities are ingested through the Yahoo Finance API with automated dividend and stock-split adjustments. Pakistan Stock Exchange records are retrieved from the official PSX Data Portal (DPS) with secondary fallback endpoints. Price histories are serialized in Apache Parquet columnar storage with background cache warming on boot, lowering simulation response times to under 30 milliseconds. If remote services are unreachable, a built-in client-side WebAssembly and TypeScript vector engine computes correlated Cholesky simulations directly within the browser session.
